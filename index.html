Quezon City Science High School, batch 1975


Hi everyone. It looks like this website and pictures can be shared on the Internet for free (at this time).
I enjoyed looking at our pictures and messages on my PC.
I'm sure most/all of us will enjoy viewing the pictures shared by our batchmates.
My apologies for letting the website disappear (due to monthly/yearly cost).

The website content is from 2017 and prior years.

This is another web page about the former students of Quezon City Science High School.
Everyone is invited to contribute to this web page.

An e-mail group containing the addresses of several classmates has been set-up
as qcshs75@GoogleGroups.com. E-mail sent (by group members) to this ID will be sent to all group members.
The last email was back in 2025. So, don't be too disappointed if no email shows up.

Thank you very much to everyone for the memories.

Click on the following to see names of current members: who-is-where

(send email to the moderator qcshs75@yahoo.com describing who you are).
It may take the group moderator 1 day or so to register new members.
