Jaime: UICC (Math & Computer Science)

After we emigrated to Chicago (in 1975), I went to several 2-year colleges (known here
as junior colleges or community colleges).  In my 3rd year, I dropped out 
of school and went to work at a shoe factory where I helped in the mail room, 
and in making store signs.  I did not like the work and pay, so I went back 
to the University of Illinois-Chicago Circle.  I finished college with a BS in 
Computer Science.  Two months after graduation, I got hired as a 
computer programmer at UICC.  After less than a year, I moved to
Phoenix Arizona.  Three years later, I moved back to Chicago.  In
1985, I worked in Kalamazoo Michigan during weekdays and return to
Chicago for the weekend.  In 1990, I moved to Tampa Florida.
I have been in Chicago since 1996.  Fortunately for me, I've always
been able to find computer programming work.  These past 10 years I've been
working at temporary jobs - a lot of companies here use contractors to
supplement their regular employees.
