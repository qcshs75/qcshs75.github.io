Hi to everyone,

Special thanks to Lolo for taking the time to call and invite me to join the group.

Actually, it is a good timing I just lost my beloved "mommy" and 
undergoing a mild depression. Though I know she is in good hands, with out 
Lord, Jesus, I still misses her so much! 

Connie di ba magkapitbahay tayo! Estrella diba ang surname mo eh Bituin? 
Annie di ba tiga Project 6 karin? You know I recognize most of the female 
faces in the photo gallery. Guys not too much. 

As for myself, God has been kind to me. Giving me a good husband and career 
both in the Philippines and in Canada. If you are wondering why I did not 
say children, it is because I have none. This is by choice. Selfish no! 
"Mahirap maglaga ng bata dito sa Canada."

After highschool, I enrolled in Polytechnic University of the Philippines and 
took up Secretarial and B.S.C. major in Accounting. Not my dream career but 
I am a practical person! I know it will be easy for me to find a job while 
studying if I take Accounting. What I advised my students before, Job first 
before interest. While studying, I worked at the age of 17 as an Accounting 
Clerk in a mfg co. and at 19 as Executive Secretary to the VP in one of the 
offices in Makati. This was the most harrowing working experience I ever 
had. I was not cut out to be a secretary because I do not take orders that 
well. Then I worked in Court of Appeals while reviewing for my CPA Exam. Then 
I went to work as an Accounting Instructor at the University of the East. 
Enjoy ako! "Pagkakataon ko ng gumanti." I got my CPA at the age of 21 and 
took my MPBA and before finishing my masters I again tortured myself by 
taking up Law. I passed the Bar in 1987. I was the only one who passed the 
bar from my Sorority after a long time of non-passer. I jokingly said "Ako 
ang nagtanggal ng jinx." I was with the National Water Resources Board 
when I was taking my LLB and my bar exams. I even had the opportunity of 
being trained at the Development Academy of the Philippines. Then after 
passing the Bar, I worked for 2 years as a Sr. Attorney with Civil Service 
and then off to Canada, then of course more studying. I took courses in the 
Assoc of Certified General Accountants, Certified Management Accountants 
and accounting computer applications. Masyadong madami! Though I am 
planning to retire soon if God's permit and just party!

By the way, almost every year I am in the Philippines. Masarap doon marami 
connection at kaibigan. Actually, my family house is near QCHS and some of 
my nieces even graduated at our alma mater. Kaya when I am in the 
Philippines I always tell myself it would be nice if I could meet my high 
school classmates again.

Expected to hear from you all!


God Bless!

Cynthia
