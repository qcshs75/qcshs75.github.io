I would like to thank the Vancouver group for welcoming me to their
homes.They always tell you pasensya ka na sa maliit namin na bahay,'yon pala
lahat mansion.Imagine I visited 3 houses in a span of 11 hours-puro kwentuhan
at kainan.May kasama din na tour ng mga houses.

I was picked up by Jo and Christy at about 2pm to go to Cynthia and
Ramon's place.There we met their "baby" named Butch or sometimes called Badong
but his full name is Salvador Villa Ronquillo.He's a 3 year old rottweiler
weighing 160 pounds.Nakakatakot at first 'yon pala ang baitbait at ang daming
alam na tricks.Marunong pang magpose kaya kasama namin sa pictorial.We had
MANGO ice cream there for dessert coz we all had our lunch already.At about
4,we left for Ed and Joanne's place.We had our merienda,cheesecake, coz
nakalimutan ni Ed ang puto and kutsinta sa Seattle. :-) There we met their
three children na ang cu-cute,lalo na the little boy.Mukhang mga foreigners, di
ko akalain na anak ni Ed.hahah Gusto ko ngang imanage ang little boy nya at
gawin kong childstar and commercial model sa Manila.There we stayed until
8pm.Tapos balik ulit sa bahay ni Cynthia for dinner and sing a long.Nagyaya
nako ng 11pm coz maaga pa lakad ko the next day.Kaso naginvite si Frank sa
bahay nila so sabi ko hanggang 12 lang ako puede coz magiging pumpkin na ako.So
kaming lima,alis ulit to go to Richmond.Grabe pala ang sinasabi ni Frank na
simple house-parang model ang bahay nila sa magazine na "Beautiful Homes".We
had coffee there specially flown from Hawaii,inentertain kami ni Christy with
her songs (si Cynthia na daw ang manager nya,tapos mamimitas sana kami ng plums
or peaches pero hilaw pa.They have strawberries,tapos na ang season.Their
cherries,ang last harvest kinuha ni Cynthia.hahah Tapos may pauwi pa sa amin na
mga home made bottled salmon (gawa ni Christy) at may dagdag sakin na homemade
blueberry jam (kasi inubos ni Cynthia ang cherries).hahah Nakaalis kami ng 1am na.

I had so much fun with all of you guys.Thank you so much for all the
trouble.Sa susunod uli,ha! ;-) See you next month.I'll let you know my sched
as soon as maka exchange ako.
Attached are 2 photos muna.The rest as soon as maubos na ang film
ko.Ngayon lang ako naka-email coz kalalabas lang ng Mommy sa hospital.She's
getting better now.Ako naman,back to work kaya can't afford to get sick.
Take care everyone.
