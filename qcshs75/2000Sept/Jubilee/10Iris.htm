Dear Batchmates,

Itutuloy ko na ang kwento ko. After the program in the school, we went to Ben's place. Doon nagkwentuhan at nagpicturan. Kung sa States meron tayong CD of the 70's, sa Manila naman ay nagkantahan kami ng mga sounds ng 70's. Raul & Ed R. played the guitar and lahat ay na ki jamming. Natapos kami around 2:30AM. Tesz, Connie, Delta, Amy and Noli proceeded to Tesz place. Nagkwentuhan pero ang nagmarathon at hindi natulog ay sina Amy & Noli.

Last Sept. 26,2000, Tesz, Connie and I went to Robinsons' Gallleria and then went to Makati and met Delta. We ate in Tequila Joes and went shopping. We proceeded to Via Mare in Tektite Tower. They wanted to stay until 12midnight for my birthday but I told them that Delta has to work the next day and we have to prepare for our trip to the farm.

Sept 27, 2000 - Trip to the farm. I want to thank everybody we went to the farm especially those na nagabsent pa sa trabaho just to be with us - they are Connie, Anicia, Ariel, Ed J (OB daw), Roger & Larry A. Siyempre TY also kina Tesz (walang flight) & Lolo & Mila. They stayed in the farm until 6PM. From lunch time until 6pm kwentuhang walang katapusan. It seems that for so many years, there are a lot of stories to tell at ang dami daming gustong ishare but it seems even if we talk for one whole week is just not enough .

Sept. 29 - Despidida kay Lolo & Mila. Breaking the record ang paggamit ko ng cell phone. Sa dami ng tawag at text that day, isang araw lang ang battery at na dead na bandang 6PM(fully charged yun ng umaga ha!). The plan was to have dinner in Glasshouse in Malate then will go to Siete Pecados in Phil. Plaza, kaya lang it was a Friday, at napakatraffic, kaya eventually we ended up sa Aristrocat sa Roxas Boulevard. Ang nandoon ay sina Lolo, Mila, Gary, Delta, Connie, Tesz, Anicia, Noli, Carol at ako. Gary , thank you for the treat. After dinner pumunta na kami sa Siete Pecados. When we arrived there, nandoon na sina Ariel & Cristy(wife of Ariel). Then dumating na sina Larry a & Joyce, Ed J, Jun Malou & Jong (husband ni Malou) and Ben. Lolo gave me one set of the pictures they have taken (more than 100 yun ha!) and showed the pictures to the whole group. Then nag disco kami - doon din sa Phil.Plaza. Believe na ako sa mga kaklase natin, parang hindi tumatanda, walang kapaguran. Hindi umaalis sa dance floor. Siyempre, si Gary pa rin ang Disco King. We left the Disco at around 2:30 AM. Sabi namin uwi na kami, kaya everybody said their goodbyes. Naunang umalis sina Lolo, Mila, Larry & Joyce. Ang naiwan sa parking lot( Connie, Noli, Anicia,Tesz, ako, Ben & Gary) at nagkayayaan kumain. We ate our breakfast ? sa Bakahan at Manukan. We went home around 5 in the morning.

Nang magkwentuhan kami the next day ni Mila, she also told me na kumain din sila sa Chow King.

Oct. 3 - Ang tres dahlias ay lumakad na naman(Connie, Tesz & me). We went to Tutuban (Divisoria) at nag shopping. Connie dropped me off at my place around 6PM and have to changed for the dinner treat of Raul & Rod. Malou & Jong fetched me and went together to Trellis. As usual kwentuhan na naman and then went to Retro (sounds kasi doon ay 70's), but when we arrived, wala daw banda that night, so we left and went to a restaurant na may Karaoke. Raul, Gary, Noli & Larry A. ang humahataw sa kanta. Owie/Annie, would you believe pati si Jong napakanta nila. We went home at around 1AM, but before we left, nagplano ang groupo to go to Tagaytay on Oct. 5.

Oct. 5 - That morning , nobody called whether tuloy o hindi ang pagpunta sa Tagaytay. Tesz insisted us to go to Sonya's Garden and when I had a talked w/ my friend in college she told me that I should see the place. It was around 9AM when Tesz text me and called her that we should go even if its rather late. We were not able to call other classmates, kaya ang tres dahlias na lang ang lumabas( Gary called but he said he cannot make it, Delta is supposed to go also w/ us but because of the traffic nakapasok na lang siya sa office ng 11AM). We arrived in Sonya's Garden almost 2:30. The place is so nice, maliit lang ang garden pero maganda ang pagkaayos. The place is worth the trip. Kung sino ang gusto mag date or maganniversay diyan, the place is highly recommneded. All fruits & vegetables they serve are all organic. Isang set menu lang ang meron sila kaya no choice ka.. The salad w/ rose petals w/ the dressing is the best. Would you believe sa tuwa namin sa banyo nila ay nagpapicture pa kami sa loob.

O diyan nagtatapos ang kwento ko, kasi the next day lumipad na ako papuntang Beijing.

Guys, I'm sure marami pa rin akong kulang na ikiniwento, kayo naman ang magdagdag.

Iris
