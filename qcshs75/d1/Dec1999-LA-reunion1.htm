Dear Classmates,
Ngayon lang ako naka-recover sa 2 days of partying with the other ka-batch. It was one biiiiiig party. Hindi maubos at walang tigil ang food, drinks, kodakan, videohan, activities at s'yempre tsikahan.

This is one of the few times that you get people with distinct characters all together in one roof. Parang comedy marathon na live. Sabay involved na ngayong ang mga special ones natin- husbands, wives and kids. Me basketball shooting contest for the kids (with prizes pa), gift giving, caroling, etc.

As Jaime and Richen has reported earlier, Tela & Jesse Bituin-Raymundo were so gracious in hosting us in their "resort" home (as per tawag ni Larry Apuada sa haybols nila Tela). Hectic na nga ang Xmas sked nila, but they still opened their doors to us. Nahihiya na tuloy akong mag-request ng 2nd round of parties sa bahay nila. We practically lived in their house for two days. Tela and Jesse, thanks to your Aunt for preparing those delicious dishes.

Ito ang ibang highlights sa reunion:

Alelie- kung mahina ang puso mo, aatakihin ka sa porma na outfit niya. Tipong Catwoman ang higpit nito, really body hugging. Kaya si Jaime pinawisan ng malapot sa photo session nila. Imagine 1 mm (damit) lang ang layo ng palad ni Jimmy sa butt ni Alelie. Hindi ko natanong kay Jaime kung gaano kalambot. Tingnan sa susunod ang incriminating evidence.

Annie- magkai-ibigan kaya itong si Annie at si Cristy per minute. Pare, rapid fire sa kwento si Manang Annie. Kaya we weren't surprised that her daughter were as vivo as her. Bilib ka naman sa memorabilia niya. Most of us wrote our fond farewells in her booklet, you'd be surprised how and what we said. Mga faded photographs ng kabataan natin, mga cards from the heart, mga trinkets made special as they were handmade (kawawa naman ang generation ngayon with their ready made gifts), wierd mementoes such as Vig Maglaya's Bic ballpen na remembrance to Annie, but you should read his dedication. We hope (with Annie's permission) to scan and digitalize this memories for posterity and for everyone to see. Sayang, it may not be as tangible as the original, but we hope to share with you all this.

Bobot- dumating pormang kagalang-galang si General. Tipong ini-scan muna ang mga mukha namin (baka siguro siniguradong walang terrorist na naligaw sa grupo). Tapos chi-nek ang mga pix natin sa before and after alumni picture collection made by Dolphy. Suggestion ng isang ka-batch, para ma-locate ang mga missing batchmates natin, ipadadamput sa mga alagad ni Bobot ang mga ito ( joke lang yan, ha?)

Side Question- sino sa mga babae ang sinisingil ng limang piso para maka-attend ng weekly parties natin, sino naman sa mga lalake ang sinisingil na sampu para uma-attend ng party?

Richen- walang pinagbago. Kenkoy pa rin, pati style ng shooting n'ya hindi nagbago. Ang eldest daughter niyang si Erin is a ranked basketball player here in So.California (MVP). Her face is a pretty looking Richen na richen.

Owie- bilib ako sa memory n'ya sa mga events ng HS tayo. Ika ni Dolphy, wala bukod kay Larry & Malinda ang naging mag-syota sa atin. Pero, Owie remembered that Charlie Solo and Maridel Bautista were also involved. Sayang Owie kulang na kulang tayo ng oras para mag-k'wentuhan.

Tana- tipong pagod na pagod si Tana pagdating. From work siya and being the high peak of Xmas shopping dito, no wonder ang laki ng pagod n'ya. Sayang din at they could not attand the 2nd day of activities. As we say here, some of us have to work while everyone plays. We were given suggestions to hold our next reunions in a resort/stay-in environ so as we could be relaxed and away from the maddening crowd.

Dolphy- Pare, talagang ganyan. Malapit na tayong umabit sa end ng warranty period ng mga katawan natin. Sa susunod, maghahanap na tayo ng mga spare parts na pamalit sa katawan natin. But then again, we still followed the gladiatorial adage of -"eat, drink and be Mary (merry pala), for tomorrow we dye (our hairs). Imagine dala pa nila yung mahjong table and set, na i-snub dahil sa walang tigil na daldalan at karaoke.

Tesz- we appreciate your effort to fly over to attend the reunion. very sorry for the very late arrival ng sundo mong tiga-L.A.daw. Nasobrahan lang ito ng beer at chicharong balat ng manok, dinuguan, kare-kare, pork barbecue, chicken teriyaki, pansit palabok, ham, empanada, fajitas, etc. (Mas lamang ang beer). We were all so happy to hear about our classmates and their efforts back home. We appreciate your giving us the situation and update. We are ever so conscious of how soon our Grand Reunion is coming up. My personal thanks for the padala and the group TY's for the pandesal and queso de bola. Walang binatbat ang California cheese sa El Pato cheese mo.

Tics/Caloy/Macky/Cherith/Edna/Leilani/Marinel/May/Nina- sayang wala kayo, we missed the pleasure your company. I was so happy to see Owie (short beso-beso lang, baka barilin ako ni Rene), sina Annie and Alelie (short beso2 lang din, baka ang misis kong si Chi naman ang bumaril sa akin). I no doubt will be happy to see and share with you the good times and memories.

To the rest of the batchmates, we would all love to see everyone this coming reunions. To some people outside our batch, but of our generation, a 25th anniversary day is just another day, no different from yesterday nor tomorrow. I felt sad when I heard this. Was there a traumatic event that triggered this? What happened 25 years that brought no joy to this people? Was there no community in their school that they felt leftout? Was there no school spirit elicited that it did not imprint nor could be recalled? Was it so bad that they have this avoidant behaviour? Can it be set aside, forgotten and forgiven?

I must say it was a very good get-together. It was like very Eddie Ilarde- kahapon lamang. Hearing Aubrey being played again, catching/surprising each other in Cubao on a regular school day, remembering those Erzats science projects that really worked (hey, we knew and understand the principle involved!), the YMCA trips to Baguio, camping in Mt. Makiling, our favorite teachers, our not so favorite teachers, getting all those vitamin D's from the sun thanks to Mrs Reyes daily morning speechs (or sermons, depends on how you take it), etc. I hope to see everyone on our Grand Reunion, if 10 people could these much good memories, I wonder how a hundred more people could offer?

Missing you all,

Gary P. _
