 BOTH SPOKE, MRS AGUILAR'S MESSAGE WAS NEVER FORGET OUR HUMBLE BEGINNINGS
 NO MATTER HOW BIG WE MAY BECOME AND MS. AGBAYANI'S MESSAGE WAS BE ALWAYS
 PROUD OF OUR BEING A GRADUATE OF QCSHS.

 PERSONALLY I WAS NERVOUS FLYING NORTH FOR THE REUNION . IT WASN'T CUZ OF
 AEROPHOBIA BUT MORE OF THIS LOOKING BACK TO THE PAST. MUST BE THE
 EXCITEMENT OF A PREVIOUS REALITY OF THINGS THAT HAD BEEN AND REMEMBRANCE
 OF WHAT COULD HAVE.

 I GOT TO OAKLAND AIRPORT 1:30 PM, EXPECTING LOLO, BUT DIDN'T SEE HIM.
 CALLED MILA AT HOME, SAID CALOY WAS THERE WHO PREVIOUSLY PICKED UP
 MARINEL TAINO. I'VE SEEN CALOY'S NEW PICTURES, BUT WASN'T SURE I'D
 RECOGNIZE HIM. HE DID RECOGNIZE WHILE I WAITED AT THE CURB (SA LAKI KONG
 ITO, KITANG-KITA KAAGAD AKO!) THANKS AGAIN CALOY FOR THE PICKUP. I SWEAR
 I DIDN'T RECOGNIZE MARINEL. NUNG HIGH SCHOOL HANGGANG KILI-KILI KO LANG
 S'YA. SHE MUST NOW BE 5'4" OR 5'5", VERY LADY LIKE.

 OVER AT LOLO'S PLACE, WE FOUND HIM COOKING KALDERETANG KAMBING WHICH HE
 AND CALOY CHOPPED UNTIL THE EARLY MORNING OF JULY 1 (OVER TAGAYS OF REMY
 MARTIN, MALAMIG SA KANILA). THIS WAS TO BE A START OF WALANG TIGIL NA
 KAINAN. PINAPAITAN, DINIGUAN, ETC. OVER AT THE BIGORNIA RESIDENCE.
 CLEMENCE AT THE TIME WAS BUSY MAKING THE FLOWER ARRANGEMENTS FOR THE
 PARTY SO EACH TABLE HAD A CUSTOM-MADE SETTING, SAY MO HA?

 COME DINNER TIME IT WAS CHINESE BUFFET, IT ALL YOU CAN! MILA MUST HAVE
 READ MY MIND. BUTI NA NALANG KAHARAP KO SI ANNIE ARLEGUI NA SINABAYAN AKO
 SA DAMI NG KINAIN. AT LEAST PURO OYSTERS ANG BINANATAN NAMIN NI MANANG
 ANNIE. PAGKATAPOS BALIK ULIT KINA LOLO. MASWERTE KAMI MERONG RESORT SA
 SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA (BAHAY NINA ESTRELLA AND JESS RAYMUNDO) AT MERONG
 RESORT DIN DITO SA NORTHERN CALIFORNIA ( KINA LOLO AT MILA). I THOUGHT
 SOBRA NA SIGURO KUNG MAG-CALL PA AKO NG ROOM SERVICE. BUMUBUHOS ANG BEER,
 WINE AND CHAMPAGNE NG GABING ITO. HINDI MAUBOS ANG KWENTUHAN, LOKOHAN AT
 KODAKAN. AS JAIME SAID WE HAD TO END AT 2 AM. KEYWORD IS "HAD TO END"
 KASI 8 AM ANG TOUR OF SAN FO NAMIN. THANK YOU MILA FOR GETTING MARRIOT
 FOR TICS, JAIME AND ME. IT WAS VERY COMFORTABLE, TICS HAD THE FOLD OUT
 BED, JAIME THE ROLL-IN BED AT AKIN YUNG KING SIZE BED. THIS WAS ACCORDING
 TO WEIGHT AND SIZE ( AYAW LANG YATA NI JAIME TUMABI SA AKIN!!)

 WE HAD NO IDEA WHERE LOLO WAS TAKING ME, AMY VILLAREAL, ED ABAN/TALITHA,
 JAIME AND MARINEL. WE STARTED OFF AT GOLDEN GATE BRIDGE, IT WAS VERY
 COLD. PERO COWBOY NAMAN KAMI, LALO SI AMY NA NAKA-SHORTS AND SHIRT LANG.
 TIPO BANG TAMPA, FLORIDA-STYLE.

 NEXT STOP WAS MUIR WOODS STATE PARK. SOBRA KASING  PORMA (NA LEATHER
 SHOES PA!) MEDYO SUMAKIT ANG PAA AKO. TIPO BANG DAPAT PANG HARABAS NA
 SHOES ANG GAMIT DITO. IT WAS A GOOD PLACE AS I WAS REMINDED OF MY STUDENT
 ASSISTANT DAYS KO SA U.P., COLLECTING PLANT SPECIMENS AND MEMORIES OF
 ILLEGALLY COLLECTED ORCHIDS FROM THE MOUNTAINS. THIS TRIP WAS REALLY
 BRINGING ON LOTS OF MEMORIES

 BY THE TIME WE GOT TO FISHERMAN'S WHARF, NAGHIHIMAGSIK NA ANG AKING MGA
 BULATE SA T'YAN. WE ALL WANTED NEW ENGLAND CLAM CHOWDER AT SANG KATUTAK
 ANG DAMI NG MGA TAO. IBA-IBA ANG MGA SALITA, ME GERMAN, RUSSIAN, INTSIK,
 HAPON, ILOCANO, ETC. WE FINALLY GOT OUR LUNCH. WE THEN PICK UP MS. TESZ
 MENDOZA IN SAN MATEO. SUKI YATA SI TESZ NG GOLDILUCKS. KA DAMI NG DALA
 NIYA. THIS IS VERY MUCH APPRECIATED HERE IN THE U.S. AS UBE HOPIA AND
 GOOD CRISPY DILIS IS HARD TO COME BY OR TAGA ANG PRESYO DITO. BALIK ULI
 KAMI KINA LOLO.

 TIPONG NAPAGOD KAMI, TULOG NG ISANG ORAS. AT 5 PM WE PICKED UP MAY
 ULANDAY. STILL THE EXUBERANT PERSON THAT SHE IS, DALWANG KOTSE KAMI, SO
 SILA NI JAIME NA SINGLE SA ISANG KOTSE, KAMI NI TICS SA DODGE INTREPID.

 WE GOT QUITE EARLY, AT DUMATING NGA RIN NG MAAGA SINA MRS. AGUILAR. OUR
 EYES AT INSTANT RECOGNITION KAAGAD. WE LEARNED EARLIER THAT SHE MAY HAVE
 RESERVATIONS ABOUT COMING TO AN OCCASION LIKE THIS AS SHE MAY BECOME
 EMOTIONAL, SHE HAD NO IDEA THAT SHE WAS HAVING DINNER WITH US. ANG
 GIMMICK NI PATTY WAS THEY WERE JUST HAVING A FORMAL DINNER. WE LEARNED
 FROM PATTY (THRU MILA B.) THAT OUR BATCH HAS A SPECIAL PLACE IN HER HEART
 AS WELL AS THAT OF BATCH 72.

 SO AFTER ALL THE WALANG TIGIL ANG KUMUSTAHAN AT HELLOS, WE WERE ABOUT TO
 START THE SHOW. LOLO PREPARED A POWERPOINT PRESENTATION OF THE PICTURE
 COLLECTION FROM THE BATCH PERO WALANG TECHNICIAN AVAILABLE SA MAESTRO'S.
 HE HAD TO RIGGED IT ALMOST SINGLE-HANDEDLY TO THE PLACE'S AUDIO-VISUAL
 CONNECTION. WE DIDN'T GET TO START THE PROGRAM 'TIL 8 PM (DI BALE PINOY
 NAMAN TAYO, SANAY SA LATE KAHIT DITO SA AMERICA). ANNIE PRACTICALLY HAD
 TO DRAG ME FROM THE KITCHEN (HINDI AKO TUMITIKIM NANG PAGKAIN, TUTULONG
 ALNG AKO KAY LOLO).

 WE HAD THE BALL GOING. YES, MRS AGUILAR WAS A BIT SENTIMENTAL, MS.
 AGBAYANI WAS INSPIRING, LOLO FINALLY HAD THE PRESENTATION GOING. WE DID
 PRESENT A PLAQUE TO MILA FOR THE TREMENDOUS EFFORT SHE DID IN BEHALF OF
 THE CLASS, DOWN TO THE LAST DETAIL OF PROVIDING CORSAGES FOR THE BOYS. WE
 DID ALSO PRESENTED A PLAQUE TO JAIME FOR COORDINATING THE EMAIL GROUP
 FROM THE START, OTHERWISE WE IN THE DIFFERENT PARTS OF THE GLOBE WOULD BE
 IGNORANT OF EACH OTHERS EXISTENCE, MISS OUT ON A ONCE IN LIFETIME EVENT
 AND GO ON WITH OUR EARTHLY LIVES NEVER KNOWING WHAT HAS HAPPENED TO OUR
 CONTINUING PAST (TO PARAPHRASE RENATO CONSTANTINO). A CLOCK OF
 APPRECIATION WAS ALSO PRESENTED TO THE BIGORNIA TO REMEMBER THIS TIME
 (DID YOU GET IT? CLOCK TO REMEMBER THIS TIME?)

 AFTER ALL THE YAKITY-YAK, WE PARTIED!!!!! CLEMENCE MANUEL STARTED OFF THE
 NIGHT WITH 2 SPECIAL SONGS THE FIRST ONE A LATIN NUMBER WHICH GOT
 EVERYONE TO THEIR DANCING FEET FOLLOWED BY "DANCING QUEEN" BY ABBA. I
 DON'T ABOUT YOU OTHER GUYS, BUT I PARTIED LIKE HECK! HINDI KO LANG
 MAI-LAMBADA SI ALELIE AT NAKABANTAY SI TESZ M. WITH HER CAMERA
 (BESTFRIEND SILA NG MRS. KO). EVERYONE DANCED FROM 9 PM TO 1 AM. KAHIT SI
 64 YR OLD MRS AGUILAR WAS DANCING AWAY. WE WERE TREATED TO SOME SONG
 NUMBERS FROM EDNA'S SON, ANNIE'S AND ED'S DAUGHTERS AND MORE SWING,
 BOOGIE, CHA CHA, LINE DANCING AND MASKI POPS. SINGING HAPPY BIRTHDAY TO
 CHERITH AND THAT SPECIAL ENDING SONG " FAREWELL TO YOU MY FRIENDS".....

 AT NATAPOS ANG YUGYUGAN, GINUTOM SI MS. ULANDAY. NAGHANAP NANGMAKAKAIN.

 NA-NAMAN KAMI INABOT.

 SARAP NG TULOG KO NG GISINGIN AKO NI JAIME. "PARE 10:30 NA, WE ARE
 HELPING LOLO AND CALOY WITH THE SET UP IN THE PARK" WE WERE SUPPOSED TO
 BE THERE AT 11 AM, BUT ACTUALLY GOT THERE AT 12 NOON. NOBODY WAS STILL
 AROUND SO WE FELT OK, UNA KAMI! SOON EVERYONE ARRIVED WITH TONS OF FOOD.
 (TIPONG THEME NG CELEBRATION WAS EAT, DRINK AND BE MERRY!) WE GOT THE
 BARBECUE GOING, TELA BROUGHT HER FAMOUS EMPANADA ( A HIT LAST DEC'S
 PARTY), JULIA AND LOI BROUGHT HUGE FRIED AND FRESH FISH, LOTS OF MANGOES,
 ETC. AS USUAL I BROUGHT MY APPETITE.

 BUT IT WAS TIME FOR ME TO LEAVE. KAMING MGA MANLY MEN DO NOT SHED TEARS
 IN PUBLIC, BUT I ALMOST DID WHEN IRIS ASKED ME TO STAY LONGER. I JUST
 SAID MY TICKET WONT ALLOW IT, BUT IN TRUTH I HAD TO GO BACK TO WORK. IT
 WAS A TOUGH DECISION TO MAKE. SPECIALLY WHEN YOU SEE SOMEONE IN YOUR
 DISTANT PAST. I'VE WATCHED MOVIES DEALING WITH HIGH SCHOOL REUNIONS,
 MAINLY THEY WERE FUNNY, BUT SOMEHOW I FEEL THE SERIOUS SIDE OF IT.

 THE EVENTS THESE PAST FEW DAYS WENT ON AND ON IN MY MIND. NOBODY IS MY
 OFFICE, AS ITS 4TH OF JULY HOLIDAY. I HAVE WORK TO DO. ITS VERY QUIET. IT
 WAS VERY HARD CONCENTRATING ON MY WORK! WORKING ON MY MAJOR DEPRESSION
 VS. DEPRESSIVE DISORDER CASE SHOULD HAVE BEEN EASY, BUT MY MIND WANDERS
 OFF TO THAT THREE GREAT DAYS OF FELLOWSHIP AND COMMUNION.

 AGAIN I THANK EVERYONE FOR BEING THERE IN PERSON AND IN SPIRIT. SPECIAL
 THANKS TO LOLO, MILA, CALOY AND CLEMENCE FOR A MOST WONDERFUL JOB OF
 HOSTING US IN SAN RAMON. I THINK WE SHOULD THANK TELA AND JESS RAYMUNDO
 ALSO FOR SETTING THE TONE OF THE REUNIONS STARTING WITH LAST DECEMBER'S
 REUNION AT THEIR PLACE AND TO YOU ALL MY DEAR CLASSMATES, THANK YOU FOR
 MAKING NEW MEMORIES. IT HAS MADE THE NEXT 25 YEARS WORTH LOOKING FORWARD
 TO. I'LL BE KEEPING  MY CORSAGE, FOR IT WAS CLOSE TO MY HEART WHICH WAS
 BEATING WITH JOY SEEING ALL OF YOU!!!!

 I LOVE YOU ALL,
