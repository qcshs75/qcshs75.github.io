 The QCSHS Class &#8217;75  celebrate our 25th year (silver Jubilee)
 together with the warm thoughts of our classmates overseas, especially
 those who passed on well before their time and hoped fervently to
 their last moments to attend this gathering.  There were 120 of you
 who survived the ordeal and emerged victorious with your well-deserved
 diplomas.  Eventually, many of you became part of some establishment
 you&#8217;ve fought against, holding positions in the government, some
 became proud leaders in the academe or private industry.

 The attendees who arrived early (Gary, Jaime, Cherith, Owie, Alelie,
 Marinel, Amy, Ed A, Annie, Iris, Caloy , Evelyn Pia and Mitch (plus
 the 2 kids),  Lolo and Mila  had dinner to an &#8220;Eat All You
 Can&#8221; Buffet.  Classmates who had not seen each other for years
 began catching up with news about other classmates who were unable to
 attend&#8230;for short  &#8220;tsismisan ba&#8221;.
 They reminisced about the good old days and exchanged information
 about classmates they have kept in contact with.

 Following day was hectic, some went on tours and other who were left
 behing helped out in making the table centerpiece, (Cherith, Owie,
 Arianne, Mila).  On the other side of the county is Clemence doing the
 corsage and buttoneer for the Jubilarians.

 Night time came and everyone started coming.  First  was Alelie,
 helping the kids put the Blue and White balloons, Then came, Gary,
 Jaime, Tics, Annie, Ray, Arianne, and of course the Special Guest,
 Mrs. Diosdada Aguilar, together with daughter, Patty and Grady (
 husband)&#8230;..then everybody started coming.  Hugging, kissing,
 embracing, shaking hands&#8230;.O mga &#8220;singles&#8221; (male or
 female) ilan ang nayakap at nahalikan (cheek only) ninyo?
 Program started with a beautiful prayer from May Ulanday.  Dinner was
 served then.  Awards were give to two people for the endless effort
 they had devoted to make this event and egroup keep going.  Jaime,
 you&#8217;ve done a wonderful job!  Keep it up and keep it going!
